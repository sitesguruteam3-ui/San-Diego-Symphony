Signature Annual Gala

AMPLIFY: A Gala Evening with the San Diego Symphony

Saturday, November 21 at Jacobs Music Center.

5:00 PM

A great symphony orchestra is a powerful act of amplification. Individual musicians come together to create something greater than themselves—performances that move, inspire, and uplift audiences. AMPLIFY, the San Diego Symphony Gala, celebrates that same spirit, bringing together remarkable artists, visionary supporters, and a shared belief in the power of music to transform lives. As a sponsor of this year’s Gala, you become part of that amplification. Your generosity is the key to ensuring that our music, mission, and legacy continue to enrich our community and inspire generations to come.

Evening Schedule

5:00–6:30 PM

Cocktail Reception
Guests will be welcomed to an elegant cocktail reception featuring handcrafted cocktails and exceptional hors d'oeuvres created by celebrity chef Richard Blais.

6:30 PM

One-Night-Only Concert
The evening continues with an exclusive, one-night-only concert featuring singer Isabel Leonard, with a special appearance by singer-songwriter Jason Mraz performing alongside the San Diego Symphony under the direction of Music and Artistic Director Rafael Payare. Created exclusively for AMPLIFY, this extraordinary performance offers guests a rare and intimate musical experience available only on this special evening.

8:00 PM

Gala Dinner & Celebration
Following the performance, guests will enjoy an exceptional multi-course dinner created by Urban Kitchen Group, led by Tracy Borkum.

9:30 PM

AMPLIFY After Party
As the formal program concludes, the celebration continues with the AMPLIFY After Party. Guests are invited to enjoy music and dancing as the energy of the evening carries into the night—a wonderful finale celebrating the community that makes the San Diego Symphony possible.

Dress Code

Embrace an evening of bold elegance and timeless glamour. Rich jewel tones, luxurious textures, and a touch of timeless sophistication are encouraged. Dress in whatever makes you feel festive and ready for an unforgettable night.

Celebration

An Extraordinary Evening of Celebration

Join us at Jacobs Music Center for AMPLIFY, the San Diego Symphony’s signature annual gala. This year, we honor Raffaella Belanich, whose generosity and vision helped make transformative Symphony projects possible, including The Rady Shell at Jacobs Park and the reimagined Jacobs Music Center. Enjoy cocktails and culinary experiences from Chef Richard Blais, followed by a one-night-only concert featuring internationally acclaimed singer Isabel Leonard and a special appearance by Jason Mraz, performing with the San Diego Symphony under the direction of Music and Artistic Director Rafael Payare. The celebration continues with dinner by Urban Kitchen Group, led by Tracy Borkum, and an after-party that carries the energy of the evening late into the night. Proceeds from AMPLIFY support the Symphony’s artistic, educational, and community programs—expanding access to music and amplifying its impact for generations to come.
